04 / FOLLOW THE COMMITTED FACTS

Post. Retry. Reject. Replay.

The authenticated local console posts a real journal, reuses its key, rejects a one-cent imbalance, changes a rule version, replays decisions, resolves an exception and inspects source health.

THE DATABASE OWNS THE WRITE

Permission is part of the invariant.

The application role can call the posting function and read facts, but cannot directly change journal rows or account balances. Constraints reject incomplete, unbalanced or currency-mismatched entries.

DATA HAS A VERSION AND A CLOCK

Unknown is a useful result.

Merchant catalog versions participate in cache keys. Synthetic feed monitoring distinguishes missing, stale, anomalous and future-dated observations; recent ingestion does not imply fresh source data.

THE LIMITS ARE PART OF THE DESIGN

A local proof, with clear gaps.

Persistent public backend hosting, tenant isolation, accounting policy, FX, backup/restore, replicated failover, distributed tracing and large-history replay remain unfinished or unmeasured.

Original software. Synthetic records only.

Tally does not move money, connect to financial networks or provide financial advice. Hashes detect modification relative to trusted state; a database administrator rewriting the complete chain is outside that boundary. The public site hosts the recording and evidence.
